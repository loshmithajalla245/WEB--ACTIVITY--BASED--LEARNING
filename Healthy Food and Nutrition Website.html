Welcome to Healthy Food Hub

Learn about nutritious foods and healthy eating habits.

Good food helps us stay active and healthy.

Healthy Foods

🍎

Fruits

Fruits provide vitamins, minerals and fibre.

Examples: Apple, Banana, Orange

🥦

Vegetables

Vegetables provide essential nutrients.

Examples: Carrot, Spinach, Broccoli

🥛

Dairy Foods

Dairy foods provide calcium and protein.

Examples: Milk, Curd, Paneer

🥜

Nuts and Seeds

They provide healthy fats and nutrients.

Examples: Almonds, Peanuts, Seeds

Daily Meal Plan

🌞 Breakfast

Idli, dosa, oats or fruit with milk.

☀️ Lunch

Rice or roti, dal, vegetables and curd.

🌙 Dinner

Chapati, vegetables and dal or paneer.

Healthy Eating Tips

💧 Drink enough water every day.

🥗 Eat a variety of fruits and vegetables.

🥚 Include protein-rich foods in your meals.

🌾 Choose whole grains such as oats and millets.

😴 Get enough sleep and stay physically active.
